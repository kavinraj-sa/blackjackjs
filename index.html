How to Play

Start a game to receive two random cards. Choose NEW CARD to draw again while your game is active.

  • Try to get a total of 21, or as close to it as possible.
  • If your total goes over 21, you bust and the game ends.
  • In this game, reaching exactly 21 is Blackjack and stops you from drawing more cards.

How Blackjack Works in This Program

Each card is represented by a number: cards 2–10 keep their value, Jack/Queen/King count as 10, and an Ace counts as 11. The program adds the card values into your sum. It uses isAlive to track whether you can draw and hasBlackJack to stop draws after 21. This is a simplified Blackjack game: it does not include a dealer, betting, or the option to count an Ace as 1.
